Common questions

Is JSON XML Editor free?

Yes. Every tool is free and you don't need an account.

Do you keep the data I paste?

Formatting, validation and Repair happen in your browser. Conversions and sorting are processed on our server and not saved. Share Drop holds your content in memory for a short time so the link works. The privacy policy has the full picture.

Is there an API?

Not a public one yet. If you'd like to use the converters from your own code, email us with the business inquiry option and tell us what you need.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, formatting, minifying, validation and Repair keep working without a connection. Sorting, conversions and Share Drop need the server.
